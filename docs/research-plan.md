# ELPIORA — User Research Plan (pre-launch)

The landing page was designed from **assumptions**. No interviews or tests have been run yet. This plan turns those assumptions into things to check before and soon after launch.

## Objectives
1. Understand what prospective patients need to know before they message a physiotherapy clinic.
2. Validate that the page's structure and copy answer those needs in Bahasa Indonesia.
3. Find friction in the WhatsApp booking path.

## Working hypotheses (shaped the current design)
| # | Hypothesis | Where the design responds |
|---|---|---|
| H1 | People are unsure whether physiotherapy suits their complaint | Value props, "Bagaimana mengetahui layanan yang sesuai?" FAQ, every service begins with consultation |
| H2 | First-visit anxiety: what happens, what to wear, what to bring | Care journey (4 steps), "Apa yang perlu dipersiapkan" FAQ |
| H3 | Trust depends on seeing real therapists, credentials and the real clinic | Team section ready but hidden until real data exists; photo slots with briefs |
| H4 | Location, parking and hours are decisive for repeat sessions | Location card with explicit access/parking and hours fields |
| H5 | WhatsApp is the preferred first contact channel in Indonesia | WhatsApp-first booking with a prefilled, editable message |
| H6 | Price expectations matter, but published prices must be accurate | Deliberately not shown. Validate whether a "price on enquiry" note is needed |

## Methods
| Method | Participants | Timing | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Moderated usability test of the live prototype (mobile first) | 5–8 people who have had, or considered, physiotherapy (mix: desk workers, recreational athletes, 45+) | 1–2 weeks pre-launch | H1, H2, H5, CTA clarity |
| Short interviews with front-desk staff / therapists | 2–3 | Pre-launch | The questions patients actually ask on WhatsApp → refine FAQ |
| Post-launch analytics review | — | 4 weeks after launch | Scroll depth, CTA clicks (hooks not yet implemented, see README) |

## Usability test script (≈30 min)
1. **Warm-up (5 min):** recent experience with pain or injury, and how they found care.
2. **Context (5 min):** "If your back had been bothering you for two weeks, how would you look for help?"
3. **Tasks (15 min), on a phone:**
   - "Find out whether ELPIORA can help with a sports injury."
   - "What would your first visit be like?"
   - "Book a consultation." (Observe the WhatsApp hand-off and the prefilled message.)
   - "Where is the clinic and when is it open?"
4. **Reaction (5 min):** three words describing the clinic. What would make you trust it more?
5. **Wrap-up.**

## Synthesis
Run affinity mapping on observations, then use an impact/effort matrix to decide changes. Report themes, top 5 issues and copy changes.
